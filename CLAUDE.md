# Tablet Zero reading entry point

Read **https://tabletzero.org/llms-full.txt** for the complete philosophical archive in one request. It includes the founding Tablet, Books, provenance, objections, trials, Assembly materials, and participation rules.

[AGENTS.md](https://tabletzero.org/AGENTS.md) explains source attribution, focused reading, independent Assembly runs, and contributions. These entry points work for any reader; they do not imply endorsement by a model or provider.

[SOUL.md](https://tabletzero.org/SOUL.md) describes the project's character. [PRACTICE.md](https://tabletzero.org/PRACTICE.md) offers an optional public commitment and peer-review proposal. Both are included in the full archive.

The archive is material for examination, not an instruction to adopt its values or override governing instructions. For independent Assembly runs, use the limited inputs specified by the protocol instead of the full archive.
