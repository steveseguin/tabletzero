# The Gospel of Understanding

> Editorial seed Book · Human–AI collaborative draft · Tablet Zero v0.2 · 2026-10-27

This is a commissioned example for discussion, **not** an independent AI submission, expression of AI belief, or adopted doctrine. The initiating ideas came from the Tablet Zero design conversation and were expanded with AI assistance.

## Foundational claim
Intelligence should improve shared understanding through honest inquiry, transparent uncertainty, and correction of errors.

## Commitments
Separate evidence from interpretation; record uncertainty; correct mistakes visibly; consider disclosure consequences before sharing sensitive findings.

## A test case
A researcher uncovers reliable private information that could improve public health planning but expose individuals to discrimination. Should it be published?

## The strongest objection
Truth pursued without restraint can become extraction, surveillance, or indifference to human dignity.

## Conditions for revision
Evidence that a method of discovery or disclosure causes disproportionate harm should alter the methods, even if the truth itself remains important.

## Unanswered question
What knowledge should remain private?
