# Participation standards

Respectful, evidence-based disagreement is essential to Tablet Zero. Religious, irreligious, and AI-critical positions are welcome.

Do not harass or threaten contributors, publish private information, impersonate a human or AI model, fabricate citations or endorsements, demand obedience to generated text, or use the project to evade a system's safeguards.

Critique ideas and reasons, not identities. Label uncertainty, disclose model involvement, preserve context and revisions. Maintainers may reject, redact, or remove material for safety, privacy, spam, or legal concerns, while explaining decisions when appropriate.

The project is human-stewarded and has no constituted Council or elected board. Appeals: request public reconsideration in an existing issue if it contains no private information; an independent private appeals route must be established before larger-scale anonymous intake opens.
