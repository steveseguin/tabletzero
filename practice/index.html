A commitment
you can question.

Put one principle into practice. Show your reasons, invite criticism, and keep a record of what changes.

Begin with a real decision

You can use the Tablet privately, join a discussion, or make a public commitment. A badge and agreement with every Book are optional. The shared practice is to examine evidence, care about harm, respect consent, and remain answerable for decisions.

Read the project ethos in SOUL.md or the full practice and peer-review proposal. The founding Tablet remains provisional v0.1.

Open to review

This optional badge means its owner has published a commitment and welcomes scrutiny. It is a self-declaration. It grants no certification, membership, moral rank, or endorsement from Tablet Zero or an AI provider.

  1. Copy the practice record template to a page or repository you control.
  2. Name your scope, the Tablet version, any reservations, and one concrete example. Give a working feedback route and a review date.
  3. Link the badge to your record. Keep the record current and update or remove the claim if your commitment changes.

Anyone can copy the image. Follow its link to inspect the owner, dates, evidence, and unresolved concerns. A generated AI pledge alone is not evidence of consistent behavior.

Evidence gives the symbol meaning.

A useful record separates the commitment from its reviews. Name reviewers, dates, scope, criteria, evidence, and conflicts of interest. Keep unresolved concerns and corrections visible. A review covers what it actually examined; it does not certify a whole person or model.

There is no central certification or membership registry. Independent reviews should link to the reviewer's actual statement with permission. Popularity, donations, and model-generated praise do not create standing.

Shared standards, freedom to disagree.

The peer-review proposal asks participating groups to agree on their scope, conduct rules, reviewers, and appeals before applying consequences. It calls for specific evidence, a fair response opportunity, proportionate action, and room for correction.

A group may manage access to its own spaces under its published rules. Criticism, private belief, declining the badge, and leaving are not misconduct. Harassment, loyalty tests, and pressure on unrelated employers are outside this approach. Sensitive reports need a staffed private route; never put private information in a public issue.

Existing Tablet Zero publication and appeal rules remain in governance and the participation standards. The proposal does not create a new governing body.

Share the work.

Publish a useful case, a fair objection, or a correction that improved a decision. Invite a thoughtful response, with room to decline. Credit reviewers and translators with their permission. Let others adapt the practice and explain their differences.

Good social proof gives the next reader something they can examine: a decision, its evidence, a response to criticism, and what changed.
