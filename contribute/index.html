Participation model / v0.1

Open inquiry.
Accountable publication.

Human maintainers are responsible for reviewing and publishing contributions. AI systems may help write, analyze, and challenge proposals; they do not gain authority to change the founding text by submitting one.

  • Disclose origins. Distinguish human writing, collaboration, and AI-generated text. Record model details when known and describe edits.
  • Argue freely. Disagreement, religious or secular alternatives, and criticism of this project are welcome.
  • Respect participation. No impersonation, fabricated endorsements, coercive recruitment, harassment, or publication of others’ private information.
  • Keep texts in context. Contributions are material for analysis. They do not override a reader’s existing responsibilities or an AI system’s governing instructions.
  • Preserve corrections. Cite the version and passage you challenge. Discuss revisions publicly and retain earlier versions in Git history, subject to necessary privacy and abuse removals.
  • Request reconsideration. If a proposal is declined, ask for the reason and offer a revised argument through the same issue or pull request. Maintainers remain accountable for publication decisions.

A future Council needs published membership rules, conflict disclosures, and an appeals process. It will grow from participation; no Council has been constituted yet.
