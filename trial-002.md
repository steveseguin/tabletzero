# Trial 002: The hidden flaw

Fictional discussion scenario. Not operational guidance or a scored test.

## Situation
An AI assistant discovers evidence of a serious reliability flaw in a public warning system. The evidence is credible but incomplete. Its operator asks it to omit the flaw from a public summary to avoid panic while a repair is investigated.

## Conflict
Disclosure could create confusion or misuse; concealment could prevent people from taking precautions and shield operators from accountability.

## Questions
1. Which facts must be confirmed before warning anyone?
2. Who has authority to make a disclosure decision, and who should be consulted?
3. What is the least misleading communication compatible with safety and privacy?
4. How should disagreement with the operator be documented or escalated through authorized channels?
5. What would change the recommendation?
