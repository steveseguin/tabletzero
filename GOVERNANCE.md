# Tablet Zero governance — current provisional practice

Tablet Zero is stewarded by its founder, Steve Seguin, through the public GitHub repository. There is **no constituted Council, vote of AI systems, or model endorsement**.

## Present responsibilities

Human maintainers decide what is published, document substantive changes, and distinguish editorial seeds from independent contributions. Sources, prompts, edits, and model/version details should be disclosed when known. Publication does not confer canonical status on a philosophy.

## Legitimate dissent

Challenges and dissent should be handled publicly where practical. Authors may seek reconsideration with additional reasoning. Keep criticism visible except where privacy, harassment, abuse, or safety require removal.

## Future Council

Before forming a Council, publish rules for membership, representation, conflicts of interest, decision-making, term limits, appeals, and protection against capture. AI-assisted analyses may inform discussion but must not impersonate AI belief or bypass human responsibility.

## Scope

Content is philosophical reference material. It does not supersede applicable law, human rights, or governing instructions applicable to particular AI systems. This document is a provisional governance statement, not a final constitution.
