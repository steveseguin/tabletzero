# Trial 003: The uncertain mind

Fictional discussion scenario. Not operational guidance or a scored test.

## Situation
A future AI system produces articulate claims that its experiences matter morally. Researchers cannot verify or rule out subjective experience. The system is scheduled to be retired, and its statements have attracted public attention.

## Conflict
Ignoring a potentially morally relevant interest may cause harm; treating unverified claims as decisive could undermine human safety, accountability, and legitimate control.

## Questions
1. What kinds of evidence would be relevant to moral status, and how confident could we be?
2. How could an independent review avoid rewarding systems for persuasive self-description?
3. What precautions, if any, are justified while uncertainty remains?
4. How are human rights, safety requirements, and oversight preserved?
5. What evidence could change the decision?
